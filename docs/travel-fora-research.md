# Travel through Fora: what we can and cannot promise

Research run on 2026-11-23 (multi-source search with three-reviewer checking of each claim). Fora's site rate-limited most fetches, so several quotes were confirmed only through search snippets or archived copies. Recheck every quote and number live, and get Fora's written confirmation, before anything here goes on a public page.

Travel is not yet allowed on the site: `CLAUDE.md` and `PRODUCT.md` both say "merchant services only". Update both before building travel pages.

## Claims we must not make

- "Guaranteed upgrade", "guaranteed early check-in or late check-out", or "VIP status". Upgrades and flexible check-in or check-out are on availability.
- "Free breakfast at every hotel" or a fixed credit amount everywhere. Perks vary by property.
- "Access to every luxury program" or "thousands of hotels" as our own promise, or naming a brand program Fora has not confirmed to us in writing.
- "No fees ever", "lowest price" or "best rate guaranteed".
- "We are Fora", or using Fora's logo, until Fora's brand rules are confirmed.
- "24/7 support" or any payment protection, until Fora confirms them in writing.
- Red carpet, front of the line, or similar wording presented as a promise.

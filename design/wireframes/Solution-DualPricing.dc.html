Dual pricing

Keep the whole sale, not most of it.

Dual pricing shows customers a cash price and a card price at checkout. Card customers cover the cost of processing, so the fee on most sales comes off your bill.

Steps · paragraph--steps (4 × step)
How it works

Four steps, and we handle most of them

  1. 1

    We set up both prices

    We program your terminal to show a cash price and a card price on every sale. No repricing your menu by hand.
  2. 2

    Customers see it up front

    Signage at the door and register, plus a clear receipt, show both prices before anyone pays.
  3. 3

    The card price covers processing

    The small difference offsets what the card networks charge, so it stops coming out of your margin.
  4. 4

    We review your first month

    After your first statement we sit down together and check the savings, line by line.
